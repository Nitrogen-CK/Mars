Neither deck edges nor the bone railing composition can be assessed. A localized bridgehead practical/light and an equivalent player-height recapture would resolve this; this review does not justify a global lighting change or new route geometry.

**ROOM_rimN and ROOM_ford-e verify overhead landmarks, not their walking approach.** Actual source-fitted camera data explains the views: rimN eye(43.4592,56.1087,1.4), target(40,55.5,4); ford-e eye(83,49,1.15615) on source ramp0, target(104,50,16). They point upward and contain little/no visible floor. Supplemental forward/horizontal player views are needed before accepting rim/stair/ford traversal continuity; the images do not prove blocked or unsupported routes.

**Lighting hides much of the intended district identity.** Court's lit statue base and wing edges are visible but its central mourner figure and surrounding room bays are black. Closest Hub/Ossuary geometry is readable, while distant approaches largely collapse into silhouette. This is specifically a limitation on assessing the supplied views against their references; no general claim that darkness is unintended in this horror setting is made.

**Shared ceiling styling remains visibly different from the masonry references.** Hall/Ossuary/ford-w show thin, repeated triangular vault panels and dark crossing ribs dominating the ceiling surface. DRESS01/03 show chunky masonry arches/soffits. The current renders are coherent with one another but read as a faceted panel lattice rather than that masonry construction. This is a visual fidelity difference, not evidence of a geometry/collision failure and not a requirement to add unsupported arches.

These are actual fresh PIE pixels, not editor views or inferred renders. They prove appearance from the captured cameras only. They do not prove physical player input, collision, gate motion, ladder behavior, crouch/pack clearance, all-direction sightlines, or every cross-tile seam.
