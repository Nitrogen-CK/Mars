# Mars environment dressing kit

The props, organic dressing and tool meshes from the six crypt concept frames (2026-10-19): ossuary, sewer pipe room,
blue atrium with giant roots, warm atrium gallery, flooded cistern, sarcophagus crypt. Generated by headless Blender 5.2
scripts in this folder, imported to `/Game/Mars/Environment/Dressing` by `unreal/mars_env_ue.py`.

The contract is [env_spec.py](env_spec.py) (folders, slots, palette, pivots, budgets) and the helper layer is
[env_common.py](env_common.py) (over the cooking set's `station_common` / `food_common`). Read both before writing a
builder. The procedural tools (vines and roots, drips and puddles) have their own plans in
[../../Environment](../../Environment).

## Run

```
"C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.2\blender.exe" -b --factory-startup --python D:\Repo\Mars\Tools\Blender\mars_environment\build_env_<family>.py -- [--export] [--sheets] [--save] [--only <name part>]
```

Each builder rebuilds its family from fixed seeds, checks every mesh, prints `ENV_<FAMILY>_OK` (or `ENV_<FAMILY>_FAIL` and
the reasons), exports FBX + JSON sidecar into `D:\Repo\Content\Environment\Dressing\export` (`--export`), writes contact
sheets into `export\review` (`--sheets`) and saves `D:\Repo\Content\Environment\Dressing\Dressing_<Family>.blend`
(`--save`).

## Style

Chunky faceted low-poly with hand-cut irregularity, flat shading, colour painted per facet into the `Col` vertex colours
(RGB linear albedo, A cavity). Stone wears the kit's world-aligned biome stone through the `EnvStone` slot. Breakup
(per-instance hue and value, world-space grime, moss on top faces, wetness) lives in the Unreal masters, so a mesh never
bakes its own dirt. Look at the concept frames, not at photographs: silhouettes are bold and readable at 10 m, and
details are big chiselled planes, not noise.

## Families and meshes (`<Name>_Mars_SM`)

### bones (`build_env_bones.py`, slots EnvBone / EnvStone / EnvWood)
- `Skull_A` intact with jaw, `Skull_B` jawless, `Skull_C` cracked cranium, `Jaw_A`. About 20 cm long, pivot at the
  underside centre, face toward Blender -y (Unreal +Y), no UCX.
- `Femur_A` (~45 cm), `Femur_B` (~38 cm, slight bend), `Humerus_A` (~32 cm), `Rib_A`, `BoneShards_A`: along +X, pivot at
  the centre, lying on z = 0.
- `SkullStack_A` (3 to 4 skulls), `BonePile_A` (heap ~80 cm), `BonePile_B` (wide low heap ~120 cm), `BoneScatter_A` and
  `BoneScatter_B` (flat floor scatters ~1 m).
- `OssuaryFill_A`: a packed block of skulls facing out with long bones laid across between the rows, 100 W x 45 H x 35 D,
  to sit on a shelf bay. `OssuaryFill_Niche`: 75 W x 50 H x 25 D, to fill the kit's `CryptWallOssuary4m_Mars_SM` niches.
  Back at y = 0, face toward -y, pivot at the bottom centre of the back edge.
- `OssuaryShelf_A`: a 2 m wide, 2.6 m tall, 0.7 m deep bay unit from concept 1. Stone posts carry a pointed gothic niche
  and a cross relief; there are three stone slab shelves. It has no fill: fills are separate meshes or the bone-fill
  tool. `SOCKET_Bay_<row><col>` sits at the bottom centre of each bay. UCX covers posts and slabs.
- `OssuaryPier_A`: the freestanding block from concept 1 (~2.2 x 1.4 x 3 m), with bays on both long faces, gothic-niche
  posts and skulls crowning the top. Fills are included on this one (it is a hero piece). UCX is the block.

- `HoodedStatue_A` (praying hooded mourner, 1.9 m, small plinth) and `HoodedStatue_B` (head bowed, hands on a sword). They
  fit a 1 m niche. UCX.
- `StatueNiche_A`: a pointed-arch wall niche, 1.2 W x 2.8 H x 0.5 D, back at y = 0, with `SOCKET_Statue` at the floor of the
  niche. `CandleNiche_A`: a gothic recess 0.6 W x 0.9 H with a candle ledge (`SOCKET_Candle`).
- `Urn_A` (tall vase ~70 cm), `Urn_B` (squat amphora ~55 cm), `Urn_C` (lidded ~45 cm), `UrnBroken_A` (base plus shards).

  crescents, a vertical bar through it, a spear-diamond above and a point below. The groove floor is `EnvGlowSpirit` faces,
  so an instance can light it. Back at y = 0. UCX.
- `FloorMedallion_A` from concept 3: a 4 m round floor inlay (concentric rings, a diamond inside a square, a quartered
  border), 3 cm proud; light and dark stone through the `EnvStone` and `EnvStoneDark` slots (the stone master ignores vertex colour).
- `Rubble_A`, `Rubble_B`, `Rubble_C`: chunk clusters. `Plinth_A`: a pedestal for urns and lanterns.

### pipes (`build_env_pipes.py`, slots EnvRust / EnvIron / EnvBrass / EnvStone)
Pipe radius is 22 cm. Segments run along +X with the pivot at the start of the axis, and the pieces chain end to end.
- `Pipe_1m`, `Pipe_2m`, `Pipe_4m`; `PipeElbow90` (enters along +X at the origin, leaves along +Z); `PipeElbow45`; `PipeTee`
  (branch +Z); `PipeFlange` (a bolted collar centred at x = 0); `PipeCap`.
- `PipeCollar`: a plate where a pipe leaves a wall or floor (plate in the YZ plane at x = 0). `PipeBracket`: a wall strap
  holding a pipe parallel to the wall, back at y = 0.
- `ValveGate_A`: a gate valve body with bonnet and stem, sized to the pipe. `ValveWheel_A`: the wheel from concept 2,
  Ø ~90 cm, 8 spokes, rim bolts, hub pivot, spinning about X. `SOCKET_Wheel` on the valve stem.
- `PipeSpout_A`: a broken open pipe end with a lip and `SOCKET_Drip` at the mouth, where the drip tool attaches.
- `DrainGrate_A`: a 1 x 0.5 m floor grate in an iron frame, top flush at z = 0. `WallGrate_A`: a barred culvert grate,
  1.2 x 0.9 m. `StoneBasin_A`: the trough under the pipe in concept 2, with `SOCKET_Water` at the water line.
- `Portcullis_A`: an iron lattice leaf with pointed spikes, 2.4 W x 3.0 H, to fill the kit's 2.4 x 3.0 portal opening.
