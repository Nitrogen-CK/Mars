02 / LOCAL UNCERTAINTY

A familiar place, a different approach.

Three proposed authored setups share one fixed Warrens. Routes and ingredient assignments vary; this is a design comparison, not procedural generation.

03 / PHYSICAL CONSEQUENCE

Guarantee opportunity. Earn condition.

In the proposed setups, required opportunities are authored and reachable at departure. Careless hunting can damage the recipe part. Optional secrets never hold supper hostage.

Concept atlas only. Route overlays are explanations, not physical or playability proof. Two-player reader / carrier staging does not implement networking.

Geometry & renderer boundaries

The Warrens plan and cutaway use the embedded current builder manifest. Primitive transforms and approximate material colours do not reproduce Unreal lighting, CkUSF shaders, live entities, physics or audio. Other districts, arrival docks, camp transit and camp cooking are future proposals.
