How to iterate

Edit design_warrens_vN.py, then python viewer/make_layouts.py and reload. The viewer rasterizes the data the way build_warrens.py does (0.5 m cells). The walk ring is the 7 m held-lantern radius; Expedition light shows what the chefs see. Schema additions the builder does not know yet (pockets, props, water, polygon rooms, extra ramps) are listed at the top of viewer.js.
Orbit: drag rotate, right-drag pan, wheel zoom. Plan: top-down. Walk: click the view for mouse look; WASD, Shift sprint, C crouch (crawls need it), Esc to leave. Walk speed 4.2 m/s, eye 1.1 m, as in Mars_PlayerCharacter_Assets.
