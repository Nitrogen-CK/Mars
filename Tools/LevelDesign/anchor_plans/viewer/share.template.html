What this version contains

Current source and saved acceptance evidence. This file is an offline review, not a playable Unreal build.

Actual PIE before / after

Images are embedded. The material repair and later orange accent pass are separate comparisons; each uses its own matched camera views.

Geometry and evidence boundaries

Phases 1–4 established the layout, physical reservations, local mechanism behavior and Bellkeeper readability. Phase 5 has Batch A presentation locally accepted; missing environmental sounds and later B/C presentation remain open. Two-human acceptance and normal heard Bellkeeper audio remain pending. No multiplayer implementation is included.

3D uses the current builder's 4,149 primitive transforms and approximate material-family colours. Cylindrical pieces are represented by their box bounds. CkUSF shader detail, imported dressing meshes, live entity visuals, physics, audio and Unreal lighting are not reproduced. The embedded PIE comparisons show the real material result. All-floor plan projection overlaps vertically separated rooms; filter elevations to inspect them.
