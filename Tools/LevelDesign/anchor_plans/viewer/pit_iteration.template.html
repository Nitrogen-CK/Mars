Pit = the central Well shaft and lower quarry read as one landmark. Base-camp transit is a boundary annotation, not a new lift implementation.
01 / Shared architecture

The Court is the Pit balcony.

A tall ruined hall opens onto the west rim through a colonnade. A root screen conceals the burial breach beyond its collapsed apse. These are adjoining parts of one place.

02 / Vertical overlap

The pump room contains the descent.

Terraces wind through its open floor into the quarry. The Belfry reader gallery sits over the service floor. Seeing a route before reaching it supplies anticipation and recognition.

03 / Threshold rhythm

Separation happens in section.

Low bone vaults, an offset screen and the root turn control reveals. Short shared thresholds change height, silhouette and enclosure; long empty corridors are not the pacing tool.

Walk the western loop

Pit rim → open Court colonnade → turn behind the broken apse → low root canopy → breached crypt wall → ordered bone bays → the same Pit, recognized from the south.

The eastern loop passes through the dry pump apron and bell service floor, climbs the internal reader-gallery stair, then crosses the crypt wall. Both are ordinary routes; the gallery shutter and quarry ladder add earned returns.
