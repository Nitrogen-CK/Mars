Open PREVIEW.html to filter, inspect full images, compare favorites and export a shortlist. Each concept has an exact prompt and a plain-language brief in plan.json. Full PNGs remain unmodified; JPEG thumbnails/contact sheets are review aids. Built-in image generation was used; no external image API runner.

Cookbook sheets each include a monster-note page and a preparation page. They share the Pickle Mimic and Tomb-Truffle Broth examples so the twelve graphic styles can be compared directly. Environment sheets pair regular concept art and a dark player-height view. Intro sheets are six-frame sequences. HUD sheets include a complete screen plus component/state studies. Mechanism sheets show physical clue/action/result relationships. Monster briefs define one simple trigger/response/counterplay loop. Each vermin board varies Pantry Rat, Sootling, Flour Puff and Lint Scuttler together, providing 12 designs per family.

The attached HTML supplies B/C/D topology and rooms: B is the descending shaft helix, C has two towers and a canal, D has two loops joined at the Well. These are mood, prop and lighting studies, not metric construction drawings; some shaft views exaggerate depth. Camera height, room scale and light reach need calibration in Unreal. Interpretations must not be mistaken for revised level layouts. Royal Daily Feast cast and the dust-creature reference supply the visual anchors. The requested floor puffs are explored as the reference image's Flour Puff family.

All story additions, recipes, behavior loops and HUD interactions are proposals, not canon or implemented mechanics. Rendered words may need a typography pass; the written notes are authoritative. No Unreal map, gameplay source, plugin or engine asset is changed by this concept pass.
