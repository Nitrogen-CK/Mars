MISE EN MONSTRE game art direction: charming spooky subterranean kitchen fantasy; ultra-low-poly flat-shaded forms built from reusable primitives, chunky silhouettes, matte solid color fields, a few bold edge highlights. Small faceless chefs with two warm ivory eyes, oversized off-white chef hats, colored sleeves and simple mitten hands. Olive, parchment cream, warm amber firelight against cool deep teal. Stylized readable stone, wood, brass and cooking props. Food-like mess, never realistic gore. NO photorealism, realistic fur, detailed skin, intricate engraved ornament, microtexture, cinematic realism or high-fantasy armor overload. Shape and silhouette carry the design. These are visual proposals, not claims of implemented content.

Create a landscape 3:2 PROTOTYPE MONSTER concept board. One large clear full-body three-quarter design, a small side silhouette, three tiny behavior beats, and a large bottom player-eye encounter view in a dark modular kitchen dungeon with a warm lantern light pool. Give the monster a memorable, simple, buildable low-poly silhouette distinct from a chef. Communicate a readable warning tell before danger, one simple trigger/response, and a visible counterplay. Show scale with a small faceless chef. Indicate eye visibility, an audible cue and a physical trace through actual images rather than dense text. Sparse short labels only. Slow dread, route choices, food/cooking associations; no realistic gore or action-boss spectacle. A monster must not permanently seal the only route.
