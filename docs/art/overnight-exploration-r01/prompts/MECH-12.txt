MISE EN MONSTRE game art direction: charming spooky subterranean kitchen fantasy; ultra-low-poly flat-shaded forms built from reusable primitives, chunky silhouettes, matte solid color fields, a few bold edge highlights. Small faceless chefs with two warm ivory eyes, oversized off-white chef hats, colored sleeves and simple mitten hands. Olive, parchment cream, warm amber firelight against cool deep teal. Stylized readable stone, wood, brass and cooking props. Food-like mess, never realistic gore. NO photorealism, realistic fur, detailed skin, intricate engraved ornament, microtexture, cinematic realism or high-fantasy armor overload. Shape and silhouette carry the design. These are visual proposals, not claims of implemented content.
