# Concept directions and play proposals

Every numbered entry is a separate visual direction. Open PREVIEW.html for images and shortlist comparisons. These are concepts, not approved lore, recipes or implemented mechanics.

## Shared direction

Chunky low-poly forms, faceless ivory-eyed cooks, flat matte colors, amber light against teal darkness. Cookbook and HUD graphics use large paper shapes, simple ink marks and sparse words. Silhouette and information hierarchy take priority over texture.

The environment studies interpret the attached B/C/D layout document. They are not metric reconstruction drawings. B descends around one shaft; C separates the Well and Belfry across a canal; D uses distinct room shapes on two loops around the Well. Light should help recognize a return landmark without revealing every destination.

The Royal Daily Feast cast anchors each intro. New incidents are alternative opening pitches. Monster loops propose one readable trigger, response and counter. Vermin roles are optional: they never consume required keys or ingredients or gate progress.

HUD studies use one active dish, four party portraits and distinct voice states. World interaction is LMB; Q/E are cookbook previous/next; LMB also closes an open book; R is the held-item throw hint. Icons should communicate state without relying only on color.

The requested floor puffs are explored as the attached reference's Flour Puff family. Each of the twelve vermin sheets includes Pantry Rat, Sootling, Flour Puff and Lint Scuttler, with different silhouettes and behavior ideas.

## cookbook

### CB-01 — Apprentice margin doodles

Loose bold charcoal-ink doodles, confident uneven hand lettering, one sage ink wash, a tiny red warning stamp and a single pasted silhouette sticker. Cream paper remains mostly empty. Monster listening bell and four cooking cards have very thick simple strokes.

Baseline sparse notebook: quickest icon read; compare against the more assembled paper systems.

Oversized friendly cut-paper stickers with visible white cut edges; monster jar, big pot, cloche and herb sticker. Rounded torn rectangles, tiny handwritten corrections, a deliberately crooked warning sticker, simple visual checklist. No realistic food painting.

Separate sticker art can be reused across recipe, bestiary and notifications.

### CB-03 — Kitchen rubber stamps

Two-ink linocut/rubber-stamp language: black chunky silhouette stamps plus olive or red progress marks. Imperfect stamped edges but huge clean shapes. Monster anatomy expressed in three shapes; cooking stages in four square stamp blocks.

Very limited palette and reusable stamps; a stamped check can distinguish tested knowledge.

### CB-04 — Grease pencil corrections

Soft dark grease-pencil circles, underlines and broad arrows on flat warm cream. Smudges are a few graphic marks, not noisy texture. Tiny chef mitten doodle crosses out heating the brine; bold squiggle steam and a marginal question mark.

The book feels used by cooks; annotations remain secondary to the four big action icons.

### CB-05 — Pinned kitchen tickets

Torn order tickets pasted into an otherwise empty notebook: one large monster silhouette ticket, two narrow sound/counterplay tickets, four sequential recipe ticket cards. Red twine corner, one brass paper pin, warm sage tape.
